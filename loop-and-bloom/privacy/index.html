Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026. Loop & Bloom ("the game") is published by After Hours Dev Studio.

What the game stores

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, Dew, boosters, lives, your garden and settings) is saved only on your device. There are no accounts, the game does not use your camera, photos, contacts or microphone, and we run no servers that receive your game data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers, approximate location (from IP address), and ad interaction and diagnostic data. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from Ad privacy choices in the game's Settings. The game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps (no App Tracking Transparency request), so your device's advertising identifier is not shared on iPhone. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. See how Google uses data in ads.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
