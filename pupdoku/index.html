Pupdoku

Find the hidden puppies 🐾

Download on the App Store See features

Nine hand-illustrated breeds hide across a colored board — deduce where each one sits.

🧩

Grows with you

Start on gentle 4×4 boards and unlock 6×6 and 9×9 as you win. Four difficulties, from no-fail Puppy mode to Hard.

🦴

Daily Puzzle

A fresh board every day — the same one for everyone — with a fire-streak to keep going.

✏️

Smart assists

Pencil notes, hints, undo, and helpful highlighting for rows, columns, boxes, and matching breeds.

🏆

Awards & leaderboards

Earn achievements and climb Game Center leaderboards for wins, streaks, and perfect clears.

☁️

Synced everywhere

Your progress follows you across iPhone and iPad with private iCloud sync.

👶

Family friendly

Colorblind-friendly labels, a relaxed no-fail mode, and puppies. So many puppies.
