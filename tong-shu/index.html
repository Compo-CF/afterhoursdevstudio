Tong Shu: Chinese Almanac

A daily Chinese almanac (通書) for iPhone, with home and lock screen widgets. Every day from 1950 to 2100 shows the lunar date, the day’s rating, what the classical almanac favours and advises against, the day’s pillars, hours, solar term, moon phase and spirits. The good and avoid lists follow the Qing imperial almanac, the Xieji Bianfang Shu (1739).
